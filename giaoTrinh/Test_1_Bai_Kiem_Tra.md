### PART A — Từ Vựng & Ngữ Cảnh *(Câu 1–8)*

**1.** A nurse greets a patient for the first time in the morning. What should she say?

- A. Good evening, sir.
- B. Good morning. Welcome to our clinic.
- C. Goodbye, Mr. Smith.
- D. See you tomorrow.

&nbsp;

**2.** Which sentence is used to ask a patient to wait politely?

- A. Please go home now.
- B. Come back tomorrow.
- C. Please sit here and wait for a moment.
- D. You are discharged today.

&nbsp;

**3.** A nurse says: *"I am Hoa, your nurse today."* What is she doing?

- A. Asking about symptoms
- B. Reporting to a doctor
- C. Introducing herself
- D. Giving medical instructions

&nbsp;

**4.** What does **"ICU"** stand for?

- A. Internal Care Unit
- B. Intensive Care Unit
- C. Immediate Care Unit
- D. Infectious Control Unit

&nbsp;

**5.** A patient has a temperature of **38.2°C**. How does a nurse read this in English?

- A. Thirty-eight point twenty degrees
- B. Thirty-eight point two degrees
- C. Thirty-eight comma two degrees
- D. Thirty-eight and two degrees

&nbsp;

**6.** Which word means **"Dị ứng"** in English?

- A. Antibiotic
- B. Admission
- C. Appointment
- D. Allergic

&nbsp;

**7.** A nurse tells a patient: *"The doctor will see you shortly."* This means:

- A. The doctor went home.
- B. The doctor is on holiday.
- C. The doctor will come very soon.
- D. The doctor is busy all day.

&nbsp;

**8.** Blood pressure **120/80** is read in English as:

- A. One twenty and eighty
- B. One-two-zero slash eighty
- C. One twenty over eighty
- D. One twenty below eighty
